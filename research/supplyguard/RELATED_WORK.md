## Contribution boundary
Reused methods: LightGBM, Random Forest, split/group calibration and ACI-inspired adaptation. Engineering: chronological data handling, reproducible benchmarks and audit records. Empirical findings: pending executed results. No new calibration theorem or algorithm is claimed. Domain-specific evaluation is a candidate contribution; novelty is not established by this limited literature check.
