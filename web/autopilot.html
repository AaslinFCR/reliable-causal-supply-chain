Alert assessment and response

Open an affected product to compare four response options. Green passes scenario checks; amber is a backup; grey is rejected; red is uncertain. No passing option means no automatic transfer.

Planning method and score explanation

Regional demand uses a seven-day average and a ±25% scenario stress band. Scores combine feasibility (35), shortfall coverage (30), history (20), operational checks (10) and fuel stability (5). This is a heuristic score, not a probability of success. Available stock, protected reserves, capacity and route closures are hard constraints. Only green main transfers execute in the simulation. Alternative stock proposals are not reserved.

Research evaluation

Frozen forward evaluation: January–March 2024, 4,965 observations. WAPE measures prediction error, not accuracy. The same-training log-LightGBM baseline achieved lower WAPE (21.86%). Forecast review remains AMBER; causal interventions remain blocked. Synthetic operations are excluded from research evaluation.

AGMARKNET provides historical market data; Delhivery provides separate historical trip data. Floods, strikes, fuel variation and warehouse consumption are demonstration scenarios, not verified live feeds.

Monitoring runs automatically while the application is running. Real operations and email delivery require connected business data and secure server setup.
