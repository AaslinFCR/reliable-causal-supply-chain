PREDICTED PRESSURE & RESPONSE OPTIONS

Regional crisis response plans

Three-day demand projections use the last seven simulated days. The ±25% stress band is a scenario assumption, not a validated probability interval. Proposed transfers respect available main stock, a 10-tonne main reserve per commodity, destination capacity and route closures. Only synthetic transfers execute.

Scored solution options and reliability gate

Green: passes scenario checks. Amber: backup requiring review. Grey: rejected or unnecessary. Red: highly uncertain. Scores use feasibility (35), shortfall coverage (30), demand history (20), operational checks (10) and fuel stability (5). Hard constraints override the score. This is a heuristic planning score, not a measured probability of success. Only a green main-transfer option can execute in the simulation; alternative proposals do not reserve shared stock.

Measured forecasting results

Frozen forward evaluation: January–March 2024, 4,965 observations. WAPE measures prediction error, not classification accuracy. The same-training log-LightGBM baseline achieved lower WAPE (21.86%). Forecast review remains AMBER; causal interventions remain blocked. Synthetic operational events are excluded from this evaluation.
