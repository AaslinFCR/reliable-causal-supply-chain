05 / CONNECT REAL DATA

Your data must come from your business

Opening stock: your physical warehouse count or ERP export. Sales demand: customer orders. Deliveries: authenticated warehouse/carrier confirmations. No public dataset can replace these records.

  • ERPNext: inventory and sales system
  • Delhivery: bulk freight service
  • AGMARKNET: market price context
  • PPAC: published fuel-price context

ERPNext and carrier accounts are not connected yet. Live orders reserve stock automatically, then wait for real dispatch and delivery events. Carrier booking, billing, invoicing, returns and cold-chain requirements need business-specific setup before production use.

Rule-based operational automation is separate from experimental causal recommendations. No predictive accuracy or real delivery is claimed for the synthetic demo.
